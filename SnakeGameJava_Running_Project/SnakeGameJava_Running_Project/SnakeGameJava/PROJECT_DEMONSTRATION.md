# Teacher Demonstration Checklist

1. Launch the program and explain the home screen.
2. Open How to Play and show controls.
3. Start the game and move in all four directions.
4. Collect food and show score and snake-length updates.
5. Press Space to pause and resume.
6. Use Restart to reset the game.
7. Hit a wall to show the Game Over screen.
8. Choose Play Again and Main Menu.
9. Open About Project and High Scores.

Explain that the snake body is stored in a LinkedList. `addFirst` and `removeLast` are O(1); self-collision checking is O(N). Food is generated randomly and checked against the snake body.
